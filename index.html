ABOUT

The 8th International Csound Conference – ICSC2026 will take place in Trapani, Sicily, from October 20th to October 22nd, 2026.

This year's conference will be held at the Chiostro di San Domenico, located in the historic centre of Trapani.

The conference is hosted by the Conservatorio di Musica A. Scontrino di Trapani, in collaboration with the Conservatorio di Musica A. Scarlatti di Palermo.

The ICSC serves as a platform for the global Csound community, bringing together artists and academics for concerts, paper presentations, keynote talks and round tables.

The 2026 edition will place particular emphasis on the work and legacy of Gottfried Michael Koenig (1926–2021) and James Tenney (1934–2006), in line with the conference focus.

We extend a warm invitation to everyone to submit proposals for papers, music and installations, or simply to attend and participate in this exciting event.
