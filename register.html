Registration information

Registration is free of charge for all participants.

Open to all — composers, researchers, developers, educators and enthusiasts. At least one author or contributor must register for each accepted submission in order for the work to be included in the conference programme and proceedings.

Further details on participation requirements are provided in the Submission Overview.

Authors

Registration is Open

At least one author of each accepted contribution must register for the conference and attend ICSC2026.
Contributions without a registered author cannot be included in the conference programme or the conference proceedings.
