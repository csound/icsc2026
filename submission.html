Call for Contributions

Submission Overview

Overview

We are currently accepting submissions of papers, music and installations, as well as workshop proposals, across all aspects of Csound.

For each accepted submission, at least one author or contributor must register.

Accepted papers must be presented at the conference in order to be included in the proceedings.

Works involving live performance require the presence of the composer, performer(s), or a designated collaborator.

Installations require the presence of the author or a designated collaborator for setup and maintenance.

Workshops require the presence of the proposer or a designated collaborator.

Authors of fixed media works are strongly encouraged to attend the conference.

Conference Focus

We encourage submissions, both musical and scientific, that engage with the themes, methods, and perspectives emerging from the work of Gottfried Michael Koenig and James Tenney.

For more information, please refer to the conference focus page.

Submission Portal

All submissions (papers, music and installations) must be made through the Microsoft CMT conference system:

https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/CSOUND2026

In the Microsoft CMT system, contributors may choose between Papers and Artistic Works, which include both music and installation submissions.

Workshop proposals are handled separately and should be submitted via email.

Call for Papers

Papers

Scope

We welcome submissions of papers covering a wide range of subjects related to Csound. The areas of interest encompass, but are not restricted to, the following:

Topics

Contributions engaging with the work and legacy of Gottfried Michael Koenig and James Tenney

Exploring Csound's educational potential

Innovations in sound synthesis and processing techniques

Introduction of new Csound features and opcodes

Development of fresh interfaces, front ends, and development environments for Csound

Real-time implementation of Csound

Utilizing Csound on embedded systems

Live coding utilizing Csound

Performer skills in Live-electronics

Integration of Csound into web-based applications

Algorithmic composition with Csound

Unleashing creativity and fostering interdisciplinary collaborations through Csound projects

Submission

All papers are to be submitted using the Microsoft CMT conference system and will undergo a double-blind peer-review process conducted by a qualified papers review committee.

The maximum length for papers is four pages, including the abstract, figures, tables, and references.

To ensure a fair double-blind peer-review, manuscripts should not contain the names or affiliations of the authors.

Submission link: https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/CSOUND2026

CMT

The Microsoft CMT service was used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service was provided for free by Microsoft and they bore all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.

Presentation

Talks: Each talk has a 20 minutes slot with 15 minutes of presentation time and a 5 minute discussion.

In the room we will provide a projector with HDMI and stereo sound via a mini-jack cable. Each presenter should bring their own laptop.

Call for Music

Music

Overview

Pieces are welcome in all styles, created using Csound. All pieces will be reviewed in a double-blind process by a qualified music review committee.

Categories

Electronic music on fixed medium

Live-electronics

Mixed pieces for instrument(s) and electronics

Electronic music with a visual component

Guidelines

Multiple pieces can be submitted by individuals, but only one piece per author will be accepted.

Please note that the maximum duration for music submissions is 11 minutes; shorter pieces may have a better chance of being accepted. Submissions in these categories should be compatible with a maximum quadraphonic setup; other loudspeaker configurations will not be available.

For pieces involving live performance, composers are expected to provide their own equipment. Available performers and instruments are listed in the “Available Ensemble” section; however, it may not be possible to accommodate all requests with the available ensemble. In such cases, composers may be invited to provide their own performers.

Submissions are to be made through the Microsoft CMT conference system. Please attach the completed music template, ensuring that all fields are appropriately filled out.

Submission link: https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/CSOUND2026

In the submission template, there is a section provided to include links to previous work or other supporting materials. Please use permanent links such as Google Drive or Dropbox, and avoid links that will expire, such as WeTransfer.

Call for Installations

Installations

OVERVIEW

Installations are welcome in all formats and approaches involving Csound. Proposals will be reviewed by a qualified review committee.

GUIDELINES

Multiple installations can be submitted by individuals, but only one proposal per author will be accepted.

Acceptance will depend on the feasibility of the proposed installation and the available exhibition spaces; proposals that are technically self-contained and require minimal setup may have a better chance of being accepted.

Submissions are to be made through the Microsoft CMT conference system. Please attach the completed installation template, ensuring that all fields are appropriately filled out.

Submission link: https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/CSOUND2026

In the submission template, there is a section provided to include links to previous work or other supporting materials. Please use permanent links such as Google Drive or Dropbox, and avoid links that will expire, such as WeTransfer.

Proposals should include relevant documentation and supporting materials.

If you have specific questions about the available spaces, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Call for Workshops

Workshops Proposals

Overview

We welcome proposals for workshops, whether software or hardware-based.

Workshop proposals are handled separately from the main submission system.

GUIDELINES

Proposals will be evaluated based on their feasibility, relevance to the conference, and the availability of suitable spaces.

To submit a workshop proposal, please send an email to icsc2026@constp.it, using “[Workshop Proposal ICSC 2026]” at the beginning of the subject line, followed by the workshop title.

Please include a completed submission form, indicating the required duration, technical setup, and space requirements, along with any relevant supporting materials. The installation template may be used as a reference.
