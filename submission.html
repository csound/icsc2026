Call for Contributions

Submission Overview

Overview

We are currently accepting submissions of papers, music and installations encompassing all aspects of Csound.

Call for Papers

Papers

Scope

We encourage the submission of papers covering a wide range of subjects related to Csound. The areas of interest encompass, but are not restricted to, the following:

Topics

Exploring Csound's educational potential

Innovations in sound synthesis and processing techniques

Introduction of new Csound features and opcodes

Development of fresh interfaces, front ends, and development environments for Csound

Real-time implementation of Csound

Utilizing Csound on embedded systems

Live coding utilizing Csound

Performer skills in Live-electronics

Integration of Csound into web-based applications

Algorithmic composition with Csound

Unleashing creativity and fostering interdisciplinary collaborations through Csound projects

Submission

All papers are to be submitted using the Microsoft CMT conference system and will undergo a double-blind peer-review process conducted by a qualified papers review committee.

The maximum length for papers is four pages, including the abstract, figures, tables, and references.

To ensure a fair double-blind peer-review, manuscripts should not contain the names or affiliations of the authors.

The submission portal will be available soon. Full submission guidelines will be published here when the call opens.

CMT

The Microsoft CMT service was used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service was provided for free by Microsoft and they bore all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.

Presentation

Talks: Each talk has a 20 minutes slot with 15 minutes of presentation time and a 5 minute discussion.

In the room we will provide a projector with HDMI and stereo sound via a mini-jack cable. Each presenter should bring their own laptop.

Call for Music

Music

Overview

Pieces are welcome in all styles, created using Csound. All pieces will be reviewed in a double-blind process by a qualified music review committee.

Categories

Electronic music on fixed medium

Live-electronics

Mixed pieces for instrument(s) and electronics

Electronic music with a visual component

Guidelines

Multiple pieces can be submitted by individuals, but only one piece per author will be accepted.

Please note that the maximum duration for music submissions is 11 minutes; shorter pieces may have a better chance of being accepted. Submissions in these categories should be compatible with a maximum quadraphonic setup; other loudspeaker configurations will not be available.

For pieces involving live performance, composers are expected to provide their own equipment. A list of available performers and instruments will be provided soon; however, it may not be possible to accommodate all selected works with the available performers.

Submissions are to be made through the Microsoft CMT conference system. Please attach the completed music template (available soon), ensuring that all fields are appropriately filled out. In the submission template, there is a section provided to include links to previous work or other supporting materials. Please use permanent links such as Google Drive or Dropbox, and avoid links that will expire, such as WeTransfer.

AVAILABLE EQUIPMENT

Loudspeakers

4 × Genelec 1030A nearfield monitors

4 × QSC E112 full-range speakers

Subwoofers available (Genelec 7360A, QSC E118)


Audio Interfaces and Mixing

RME UFX+ audio interface

Yamaha QL1 digital mixer

Additional analog mixers available


Microphones

Selection of condenser and dynamic microphones (Neumann, AKG, Shure, Rode)

Call for Installations

Installations

OVERVIEW

Installations are welcome in all formats and approaches involving Csound. Proposals will be reviewed by a qualified review committee.

GUIDELINES

Multiple installations can be submitted by individuals, but only one proposal per author will be accepted.

We are currently evaluating available locations for sound installations. More information will be provided soon.

Acceptance will depend on the feasibility of the proposed installation; proposals that are technically self-contained and require minimal setup may have a better chance of being accepted.

Submissions are to be made through the Microsoft CMT conference system. Please attach the completed installation template (available soon), ensuring that all fields are appropriately filled out. In the submission template, there is a section provided to include links to previous work or other supporting materials. Please use permanent links such as Google Drive or Dropbox, and avoid links that will expire, such as WeTransfer.

Proposals should include relevant documentation and supporting materials.

If you have specific questions about the available spaces, please do not hesitate to contact us.
